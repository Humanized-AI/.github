## Where we come from

Humanized was born inside the planning office, not the AI lab. Its roots are in
50 Hz railway electrical infrastructure — one of the most regulated corners of
engineering, where every cable cross-section, every protection setting and every
drawing must agree with a norm, a calculation sheet and an authority form before
a single train can run.

We watched certified engineers spend weeks doing work no one certified them for:
copying values from CAD into spreadsheets, from spreadsheets into Word, from Word
into submission portals — and then waiting months for a rejection triggered by
one number that had drifted out of sync along the way.

The problem was never the engineering. It was the coordination around it.

## The core idea

**The AI drafts. The engineer decides.**

Humanized reads a project's inputs together — CAD drawings, calculation sheets,
specifications and regulatory documents — as one connected model rather than a
pile of files. It checks that model against a live norm base, drafts the
compliant, fully cited deliverable, and flags every discrepancy before anything
is submitted.

What it does not do is sign. Every output is a draft with its sources attached,
placed in front of a certified engineer who reviews, corrects and approves.
Responsibility stays where the law and good practice put it: with the human.

We call this *human-centered agentic AI*: agents that take on the repetitive,
cross-document labour of engineering and planning, while the professional stays
in command of every decision that carries a signature.

## Why "Humanized"

Most automation tools try to remove the human from the loop.
We build tools that give the human the loop back — with the busywork removed,
the evidence attached and the final word untouched.

## What we build

- **groundwork** — an engineer-in-the-loop workflow engine for AEC: node-based,
  self-hosted, extensible.
- **nodes-aec** — domain nodes for CAD, BIM (IFC), calculation sheets and
  submission formats.
- **normbase** — a norm and standard index with citation-level traceability.

Railway electrification is where we started. The same coordination problem
exists in structural, HVAC, fire protection and grid planning — and the same
answer applies.
